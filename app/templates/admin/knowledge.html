How the analyser learns

Every bill that reconciles (line items add up to the invoice total) teaches the analyser its carrier's plan names, prices and charge categories. These facts are fed into the prompt for the next bill from that carrier. Bills you verify on the bill page become reference examples. Untick a fact to stop using it, or edit it to correct it.
